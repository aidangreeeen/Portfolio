// Overview

A design exploration for a pair of over-ear headphones. The initial design was modeled in SolidWorks, then rendered in Midjourney to explore materials and finish.

// Inspiration

The design draws on the resurgence of retro design, and in particular the translucent electronics of the late ’90s and early 2000s, when clear shells put the internals on show instead of hiding them. The colour comes from the Xbox green: a tinted, see-through shell that lets the drivers, screws and structure read through the surface.
