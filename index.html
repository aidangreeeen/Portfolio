03 — Background

About

I grew up in Hailey, Idaho where I spent all my time snowboarding and enjoying the outdoors. Using the right sleeping bag on a cold night, high pointing a snowmobile that an older model wasn't capable of, or flying somewhere in 3 hours that would have taken a full day in a car inspired my curiosity to understand how things function. My experience in engineering answered that curiosity but couldn't explain why some cars sounded better, certain lights made my house feel more like home, or when I looked for inspiration, it was always through the work of creatives and engineers.

How is it that everyday objects can make my life easier or add an aesthetic beauty that I didn't even know I needed? This prompted a shift in my focus to engage my technical experience with design. Utilizing my skillset to solve problems I believe demand a creative solution, bridging the gap between how things work and how they're experienced.
